What you're looking at
1
Sorted by stakes, not by arrival. Highest-amount invoices and lowest-confidence fields surface first — reviewer attention follows the risk, not the timestamp.
2
Why-it-routed is the headline. Each row says exactly what tripped the threshold: rate band ambiguity, currency missing, OCR failure. No clicking into a detail page to find out.
3
Three buttons, one keystroke each. Accept writes to report, Edit opens an inline field, Reject sends to manual handling. Every action logs the original value, the chosen value and the reason code.
