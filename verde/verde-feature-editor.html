What you are looking at
1
Provenance is visual. AI-drafted sentences are dotted. Human-edited sentences are filled. There is no hidden state, the reader can always tell.
2
Inline rewrite keeps flow. Regenerating a single section is one click, the consultant does not leave the editor and the audit log captures the swap.
3
Submit is a state transition. Hitting submit flips the report to Under Review and notifies the named reviewers. The action that ships the report is the same action that locks the audit trail.
