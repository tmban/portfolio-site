What you're looking at
1
Six KPIs, one row. Carbon, energy, water, waste and data quality sit together so a fund manager reads portfolio health in one glance, not five tabs.
2
Variance is the headline. Every metric shows movement against the prior period, colour-coded so the one bad number (water, ↑9%) can't hide.
3
Actual vs target, always paired. The like-for-like trend is plotted against the science-based pathway so progress is judged against the commitment, not just last year.
4
Data quality is a first-class metric. Coverage, completeness and automation surface on the landing view, because a number you can't trust isn't an answer.
