Feature 07 — Procurement split
Whose energy is it, and whose to fix
Energy use split by who procures it, landlord or tenant, because responsibility for reduction follows the meter. Where the split can't be resolved, the gap is shown openly and routed into gap-fill rather than buried in an average.
