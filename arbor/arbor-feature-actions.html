What you're looking at
1
Problem and answer in one card. The platform doesn't just raise an alert — it proposes the exact change, with the before / after diff in front of the user, ready to accept in one click.
2
Severity-aware chips. Critical, warn and info are colour-coded and counted. The user can triage by severity rather than waste time on a flat list of 18 items.
3
Every accept goes into the activity log. A suggestion turns into a signed event with the source rule, the confidence score and the responsible user, so the audit trail explains every fix.
