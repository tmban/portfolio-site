What you're looking at
1
The toast doesn't block. A small ephemeral confirmation appears top-right, fades on its own, and never traps the user mid-flow. Reassurance, not friction.
2
Partial entity is real. Exchange House appears in the side-nav with a "draft" badge the moment it's saved — the rest of the platform treats it as a first-class entity rather than a private form in flight.
3
Undo with a grace period. An eight-second undo sits inside the confirm panel so a mis-clicked "Save as draft" is recoverable without hunting through the activity log.
