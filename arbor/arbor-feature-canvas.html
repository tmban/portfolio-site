What you're looking at

Same anatomy at every level. Header, chips, parent path, metrics, sources, health. A space, a fund and a meter all open to this layout, so the platform feels like one product rather than five.

Metrics in context. The middle column carries the numbers the user came for — energy, water, carbon — each with its sparkline and variance, so the entity's story is readable without leaving the page.

Health is shown next to the value. Sources, coverage and completeness sit in the same view as the metrics they produce. Trust travels with the number rather than living on a separate audit screen.
